# Cyrograf mascot

The Cyrograf mascot is a friendly angel holding a quill and a sealed parchment
contract. The ivory wings and warm expression illustrate the project tagline:
“Contracts that are valid even beyond death.”

## Design brief

A cheerful angelic scribe with soft curls, expressive eyes and ivory wings,
wearing an ivory robe with a pale-blue accent. The feather quill
and cream parchment with a red wax seal keep the focus on signing a contract.
Rounded outlines and gentle shading give the mascot an illustrated character
while keeping it readable at a small size. The background is transparent.
